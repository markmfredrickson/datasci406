Module 3 Project, Part B: Check Your Numbers

This is the output of one complete run of the project, with a fixed random seed. Use it to see whether your own results are in the right ballpark: the same order of magnitude, the same conclusions, the same first digit or two. It is not an answer key. There is no code and no explanation here, and the exam asks about your project, not this one.

What should match, and how closely:

  • Exact values do not depend on random numbers and should match to every digit shown: everything in B.1(c), the jackknife SEs in B.1, and the training errors in B.3.
  • Bootstrap and cross-validation estimates vary from run to run. Your bootstrap SEs, interval endpoints and cross-validated errors should agree with these to about two significant digits.
  • Times depend on your computer.
